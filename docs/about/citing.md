# Citing D0FUS

If you use D0FUS, please cite the reference article:

> T. Auclair, E. Nardon, Y. Sarazin, J.-F. Artaud, B. Boudes, C. Bourdelle,
> J.-L. Duchateau and A. Torre, *The tokamak system code D0FUS and its first
> applications: Impact of B and confinement scaling law on power plant design*,
> Fusion Engineering and Design **219** (2025) 115270.
> [doi:10.1016/j.fusengdes.2025.115270](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fusengdes.2025.115270)

The repository also provides a `CITATION.cff` file, used by the *Cite this
repository* button of GitHub.

The models, their derivations and their validation are documented in the PhD
thesis of T. Auclair, from which the {doc}`/theory/index` and
{doc}`/validation/index` pages are adapted:

> T. Auclair, *Apport des supraconducteurs à haute température critique au
> dimensionnement de tokamaks électrogènes*, PhD thesis, Aix-Marseille Université,
> CEA-IRFM (2026).

Release v2.7.0 is the version archived with the thesis.
